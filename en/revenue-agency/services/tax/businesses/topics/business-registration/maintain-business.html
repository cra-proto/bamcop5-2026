It is important to keep your business information complete and up to date with the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA). You must notify the CRA about certain changes you make to your business.

On this page

  • Access your business information online
  • Update your business information
  • Growing your business
  • Changes to business operations

Access your business information online

To access your business information online, you need to link your Social Insurance Number (SIN) to your business number (BN) and add your BN to your CRA account.

Once set up, you can view and manage your program accounts, and authorize representatives using My Business Account in your CRA account.

Security measures for accessing business information

Only owners, partners, or directors whose name and SIN are on file with the CRA have the authority to:

  • Access the business information
  • Make changes to the business
  • Add representatives
  • Access My Business Account

How to add your SIN to your CRA business account

If the SIN of an owner, partner, or director has not been provided to the CRA, you can add it to your BN in one of the following ways:

Add your SIN online

An owner, partner, or director can add their SIN when adding their BN to their CRA account.

To add your SIN:

  1. Sign in to your CRA account.
  2. On the Welcome page, select +Add account
  3. Select Business Account, then Add existing business number(BN)
  4. Read and accept the My Business Account Terms and Conditions, if prompted
  5. Enter your 9-digit business number
  6. Answer Yes or No to the question: "Have you filed a GST/HST return or a T2 Corporation income tax return?" Then select Submit
    • If Yes, enter the requested information from your assessed return
    • Important: If you select No, you can only link your SIN if your BN was registered within the last 28 days.

If your information matches CRA records and validation is successful, your SIN will be automatically linked to your BN and your BN will be added to your CRA account.

Requests submitted through Represent a Client, fax, or mail, must be dated and signed by the owner, partner, or director asking the CRA to add their SIN to the business account.

Submit a request through Represent a Client

A representative can upload a signed request using the Submit documents service in the Represent a Client portal of their CRA account.

Fax your request

An owner, partner, director, or representative can fax a signed request to the business's tax centre:

  • Prince Edward Island Tax Centre: 1-833-724-7237
  • Sudbury Tax Centre: 1-855-276-1529
Mail your request

An owner, partner, director, or representative can mail a signed request to the business's tax centre.

For addresses, go to Find a CRA address.

Update your business information

Depending on the type of change, you may be able to update the information in your CRA account or you may have to contact the CRA.

  • Authorize or cancel a representative
  • Change of owners, partners, or directors
  • Change a business address
  • Change a business operating name
  • Access to corporate tax information
  • Change of legal status
  • Change of fiscal year-end
  • Change of business activity

Growing your business

If your business expands, there may be new reporting requirements and you may have to provide new information to the CRA.

  • Buying a business
  • Amalgamation (corporations)
  • Opening more locations, branches, or divisions
  • Bringing personal assets into your business

Changes to business operations

If there are changes to your business operations, you must notify the CRA to avoid tax implications that may affect your program accounts.

  • Temporarily stopping business operations
  • Selling a business
  • Closing CRA program accounts
  • Bankruptcy/receivership
  • Retirement or death of business owner or partner
  • Resuming operations
